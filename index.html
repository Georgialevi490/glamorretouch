About Retouch Glamor

A Nairobi house for modeling, fashion and design.

Our story

Retouch Glamor opens at Sarit Centre, Westlands, with one aim: to give Kenyan talent a professional home. We combine a model agency, a photo studio and a design team so a model can be trained, photographed and booked in one place.

What we believe

  • Professional standards on every set
  • Fair, transparent contracts
  • Kenyan faces on local and global stages
